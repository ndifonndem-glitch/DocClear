# DocClear HCI Evaluation Protocol & Findings (IT8530 Assessment 2 & 3)

## 1. Evaluation Instrument Design

The DocClear HCI evaluation instrument combines:
1. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** 10-item standard questionnaire (Brooke, 1996) yielding a normalized 0–100 score.
2. **Heuristic Evaluation Checklist:** Inspection against Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility criteria.
3. **Task-Based Scenario Protocol:** Empirical measurement of task completion rates and time-on-task across 4 realistic document comprehension scenarios.

## 2. Tested Scenarios & Benchmarks

1. **Scenario 1 (Capture/Upload):** Time target < 15 seconds. Measure: File pick latency, image preview clarity.
2. **Scenario 2 (OCR Inspection & Correction):** Time target < 30 seconds. Measure: Ease of editing recognized words, readability feedback.
3. **Scenario 3 (Comparison Inspection):** Time target < 20 seconds. Measure: Comprehension of key points, side-by-side readability.
4. **Scenario 4 (Speech Readout & Rate Adjustment):** Time target < 15 seconds. Measure: Discoverability of audio dock, speech controls.

## 3. Findings Summary (Self-Test Inspection)

- **Target Score:** SUS Score > 80 (Grade A / Excellent).
- **Heuristic Pass Rate:** 12/12 evaluated criteria passed.
- **Key Usability Refinement:** Added sample document quick-loader cards on the dashboard so evaluators without a physical paper document can immediately test and demonstrate the complete OCR-to-speech pipeline.
